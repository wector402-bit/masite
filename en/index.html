FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get the video?

It depends on the length of the video and how quickly feedback comes in. You get a precise date with the quote.

What do you need to get started?

Access to your product (or screenshots), your logo, your colours and 15 minutes for the brief. If you already have a script, we start from it.

Do you also make videos in French?

Yes. The voice-over and on-screen text can be in English or French. The Complete package includes a second language version.

What if I don't like the video?

You approve the script and storyboard before any animation, so there are no surprises on the substance. After that, the revisions in your package are included.

How does payment work?

50% when you order and 50% on delivery, by bank transfer. The Essential package is paid in full when you order.
